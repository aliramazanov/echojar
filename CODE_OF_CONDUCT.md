# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help.

## What is expected

Discuss the code, not the person. Disagreement is fine and useful. Contempt is not.

Take criticism of your work without treating it as criticism of you, and give it the same way.

If you are wrong, say so and move on. That is normal here.

## What is not acceptable

Personal attacks, harassment, or discrimination of any kind. Deliberately derailing discussions.
Publishing anyone's private information.

## Reporting

Email toaliramazanov@tutamail.com. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept private.

Serious or repeated problems end in a block from the project. That decision is the maintainer's.
